The ATS resume checklist.

Twelve checks to run before you hit submit. If a box cannot be ticked honestly, fix it or leave the claim out.

01 Make it readable

  • One column, no tables or text boxes for key content.
  • Standard headings: Summary, Skills, Experience, Education.
  • Real selectable text, not a screenshot or image of a resume.
  • Dates as month and year, with an end date for every role.

02 Put contact details in plain text

  • Name, email, phone, city, and LinkedIn at the top.
  • No contact details trapped in a header, footer, or graphic.
  • Use a professional email address you check daily.

03 Match the posting's language

  • Extract the top 8–12 must-have terms from the posting.
  • Mirror exact tool, platform, and certification names where true.
  • Use the employer's vocabulary, not synonyms, for must-haves.
  • No white text, hidden keywords, or skills you cannot defend.

04 Show evidence, not duties

  • Each bullet: action + scope + measurable result.
  • Replace "responsible for" with what actually changed.
  • Add honest numbers: team size, volume, budget, time saved.
  • Three to five bullets for each recent, relevant role.

05 Fix the top third

  • Summary: target role + years/scope + two skills + one result.
  • Core skills ordered for this posting, not alphabetically.
  • Most relevant role and bullets first on the page.
  • No photo, objective statement, or skill bars.

06 Test before you apply

  • Copy the PDF text into a plain editor. Does it still read?
  • Scan against the same posting and fix structure warnings first.
  • Re-scan. Stop when the gaps are things you cannot claim.
  • Apply while the posting is fresh, not two weeks later.
