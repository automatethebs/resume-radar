Payment guide

Pay with USDC on Base.

Pro is a one-time $9 report. USDC on Base keeps it accountless: no card details, no billing account, and no KYC submission to ResumeRadar. The site verifies the transfer on-chain in your browser.

Five steps, about five minutes.

01

Open a wallet that supports Base

Coinbase Wallet, MetaMask, Rainbow, or any EVM wallet works. If Base is not already in your network list, add it with chain ID 8453 and the public RPC https://mainnet.base.org.

02

Get USDC on Base

The simplest route is to buy USDC on an exchange and withdraw it to your wallet on the Base network. If your USDC is on another chain, bridge it to Base first. USDC on Base uses 6 decimals.

03

Pay exactly 9 USDC

Open the checkout from the pricing page or a report and click Pay $9 from wallet. The site asks your wallet to switch to Base, sends the USDC transfer, and verifies the receipt automatically. You can also send 9 USDC on Base to the address below, or use the “Open in wallet” link or QR code.

04

Confirm the transaction

The one-click flow waits for the Base receipt and verifies it for you. If you paid manually, copy the transaction hash from your wallet or find it on BaseScan by searching your wallet address.

05

Verify and unlock Pro

Paste the hash into the unlock dialog on the pricing page or in the report. The site checks the receipt, the USDC contract, the recipient, and the amount. If everything matches, Pro unlocks on that device.

Common problems

I sent USDC on another network. What now?

ResumeRadar only verifies USDC on Base. A transfer on Ethereum, Polygon, or another chain will not unlock Pro automatically. Open a support issue with the transaction hash and we will try to help, but cross-chain transfers can be unrecoverable.

The transaction is confirmed but the unlock says “not found”.

Public RPC endpoints can lag for a minute. Wait 60 seconds and try the hash again. Make sure you copied the transaction hash, not the wallet address or a block number.

I sent less than 9 USDC.

The verifier requires at least 9 USDC in a single transfer to the configured address. Send the remainder in a second transaction and contact support with both hashes.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There is no account system. Pro is stored in your browser's local storage on the device where you verify the payment. Clearing site data or using another browser will require restoring access with the same transaction hash.
