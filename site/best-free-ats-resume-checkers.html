What "free" actually means

Before you upload a resume, ask three questions. First, does the free tier require an account? Four of the five tools here do. Second, does it require a document upload? Again, four of the five do; ResumeRadar only needs pasted text. Third, what is held back? A free score is useful, but it is not the same as a free fix list. ResumeRadar's free scan includes the top five missing keywords and the first four fixes, while the paid tools generally keep the full line-by-line report for subscribers.

Why the scores are not comparable

Each tool runs different checks and weights them differently. ResumeRadar scores keyword match, parser-safe structure, measurable impact, and readability. Resume Worded uses 30+ recruiter-style checks. Teal and Rezi emphasize their own builder and keyword systems. A resume that scores 82 in one tool can score 91 in another without a single edit. Use the score to track changes within one tool, and compare the reasons and fixes across tools.

How to choose

  • You want no account and no upload: use ResumeRadar's free scan, then decide whether the $9 Pro report is worth it.
  • You want to build or rewrite the resume: use Teal or Rezi, and compare their free limits and template libraries.
  • You want AI line-by-line feedback: use Resume Worded's free score first, then decide whether Pro is worth the subscription.
  • You want one platform for the whole search: compare Jobscan's plans, especially if LinkedIn optimization and application tracking matter to you.

Frequently asked questions

Which free ATS resume checker is actually free?

All five tools have a free entry point, but free means different things. ResumeRadar gives the full four-category scan with no account and no upload. Teal's Free Forever plan includes 10 templates, top-5 keyword matching, and basic analysis. Rezi's free plan includes 1 resume, 1 AI interview, and 3 PDF downloads. Resume Worded gives a free score and the top issues. Jobscan offers limited free scans with an account.

Do free ATS checkers require an account?

ResumeRadar does not. Jobscan, Teal, Rezi, and Resume Worded are account-based products, so their free tiers require signing up. Check each vendor's current terms before uploading a resume.

Do these tools upload my resume?

ResumeRadar does not: you paste plain text and the scan runs in the browser. Jobscan, Teal, Rezi, and Resume Worded process uploaded resumes in their accounts. Their privacy policies govern how those files are stored and deleted.

Are ATS scores comparable across tools?

No. Each tool uses different checks, keyword lists, and weighting, so the same resume can score 10 or more points apart. Compare the reasons and the recommended fixes, not the number alone.

Which free ATS checker should I use?

Use ResumeRadar for a private, no-account check of one posting. Use Jobscan for a broad job-search platform with LinkedIn optimization. Use Teal or Rezi if you want a resume builder with templates. Use Resume Worded if you want AI rewriting and line-by-line feedback on the resume itself.
