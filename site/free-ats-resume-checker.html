How the free scan works

  1. Paste your resume as plain text. Include the contact line, section headings, dates, and bullets. No file upload is needed.
  2. Paste the job description. Optional, but this is what turns a generic checklist into a match score for the actual role.
  3. Read the ranked fixes. Add only what is true. Re-scan after each edit and watch the score move.

Why there is no signup

Most resume scanners want an account so they can store your document, email you, and sell a subscription. ResumeRadar does not need any of that. The scoring engine is a local JavaScript file; the site has no server-side resume endpoint. There is no account to create and no resume to delete.

The trade-off is honest: the free scan shows the first five missing keywords and four fixes. If you want the complete list, Pro is a one-time $9 unlock. You are paying for the rules and the report, not for infrastructure.

Frequently asked questions

Is this ATS resume checker really free?

Yes. The score, category breakdown, top five missing keywords, and top four fixes are free with no account. Pro is an optional one-time upgrade that reveals every missing term, the complete role checklist, all fixes, and a text export.

Do I have to upload my resume?

No. Paste the plain text and the scan runs in your browser. There is no upload endpoint, no account, and no resume storage. The only thing saved locally is your Pro unlock state if you buy it.

How accurate is an ATS resume checker?

It can measure keyword coverage, parser-safe structure, measurable impact, and readability against a specific posting. It cannot reproduce every employer's ATS configuration or predict whether a recruiter will advance you. Treat the score as a prioritized editing list, not a hiring prediction.

Does it work without a job description?

Yes. You can scan against one of 39 built-in role checklists. Adding a real posting makes the result more specific because the keyword match is built from the employer's language.

What is the difference between the free scan and Pro?

The free scan shows the top five missing keywords and top four fixes. Pro shows every missing keyword ranked by importance, the full role checklist, every recommended fix, a cover-letter outline, interview questions, and a downloadable text report.

Is ResumeRadar a Jobscan alternative?

It is a lighter alternative for one job: checking a resume against a specific posting. Jobscan is a broader paid platform with LinkedIn optimization and other career tools. ResumeRadar is free to scan, $9 once for Pro, and does not require an account or resume upload. Use whichever fits the task you need done.
