Questions

The honest answers.

Is ResumeRadar really free?

Yes. The score, category breakdown, top five missing keywords, and top fixes are free with no account. Pro is a one-time upgrade (not a subscription) that reveals every missing keyword, the full role checklist, all fixes, and a text export.

Is my resume uploaded anywhere?

No. The scan runs entirely in your browser. Your resume text is never sent to a server and is not stored after you close the tab. The only thing saved locally is your Pro unlock state.

What is an ATS, exactly?

An applicant tracking system is software employers use to receive, parse, search, and rank applications. It turns your resume into structured fields and lets recruiters filter by keywords, skills, titles, and dates. Most large employers use one.

Can this guarantee an interview?

No tool can. ResumeRadar measures how well your resume matches a specific posting and flags issues that commonly break parsing. It improves your odds; it cannot replace relevant experience, a referral, or a human conversation.

Does it work for non-tech jobs?

Yes. There are checklists for 39 fields including nursing, teaching, sales, accounting, operations, HR, design, and engineering. You can also paste any job description and the checker extracts keywords from it directly.

What does Pro include?

Every missing keyword ranked by importance, the complete role checklist, all recommended fixes, and a downloadable text report. One payment, no subscription, no account.
