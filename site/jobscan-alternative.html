When Jobscan is the better choice

Choose Jobscan if you are running a full job search and want one dashboard for resume scans, LinkedIn optimization, application tracking, and a browser extension. It is also the safer choice if you want an established brand with a broad feature set and do not mind a subscription.

When ResumeRadar is the better choice

Choose ResumeRadar if you have one posting to tailor for, you do not want to create another account, or you would rather not upload your resume to a third-party service. It is also a practical fit for technical and Web3 roles, where the keyword lists are specific and the $9 one-time price is easy to justify.

What the free scan actually shows

The free report gives you a 0–100 score, four category scores, the top five missing keywords, parser warnings, and the first four fixes. Pro adds every missing keyword ranked by importance, the complete role checklist, every fix, a cover-letter outline, eight interview questions, and a downloadable text report. There is no subscription and no recurring charge.

Frequently asked questions

Is ResumeRadar really a Jobscan alternative?

For one task, yes: checking a resume against a specific job posting. ResumeRadar gives you a score, missing keywords, parser warnings, and fixes without an account or resume upload. Jobscan is the better fit if you want a broader job-search platform with LinkedIn optimization, job tracking, and a browser extension.

What does ResumeRadar cost?

The scan is free. Pro is a one-time $9 unlock, not a subscription. It reveals every missing keyword, the full role checklist, all recommended fixes, a cover-letter outline, interview questions, and a text export.

Does ResumeRadar upload my resume?

No. The scoring engine is local JavaScript. Paste plain text and the scan runs in your browser; there is no resume upload endpoint and no server-side storage. Only your Pro unlock state is kept in local storage if you buy it.

Does Jobscan require an account?

Jobscan is an account-based service with limited free scanning and paid subscription plans. Check Jobscan's site for its current free limits, features, and prices, since those change.

Which one should I use?

Use ResumeRadar if you want a fast, private, low-cost check for one posting, especially in a technical or Web3 role. Use Jobscan if you want an established platform that also optimizes LinkedIn, tracks applications, and bundles several job-search tools.
