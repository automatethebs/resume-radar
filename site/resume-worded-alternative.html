A lighter Resume Worded alternative for one ATS check.

Resume Worded is an AI resume review platform with a genuinely useful free score and detailed line-by-line feedback on Pro. ResumeRadar does one narrower job: it checks the resume you already have against a specific posting, shows the keyword gaps, and explains the fixes — without an account, an upload, or a subscription.

The short version

If you want AI rewriting and line-by-line feedback on the resume itself, Resume Worded is the more complete product. It gives you a free score and the top issues, then charges Pro pricing for full analysis, AI rewriting, targeted resumes, LinkedIn review, and templates. If you want a private, inexpensive check of the posting in front of you, ResumeRadar is smaller on purpose.

When Resume Worded is the better choice

Choose Resume Worded if the resume itself needs a deep edit and you want the tool to suggest the rewrite. Its free review gives you a score and the biggest issues, which is enough to judge whether the paid product is worth it. Pro adds line-by-line analysis, AI rewriting, targeted resumes, LinkedIn review, and ATS templates. If you are applying broadly and want a coach-like workflow, the annual plan at $19/month is the cheapest way to use it.

When ResumeRadar is the better choice

Choose ResumeRadar if you do not want to create an account or upload a document, and you mainly need to know whether the resume you already have answers one posting. Paste the text, get the score and keyword gaps, fix the highest-leverage items, and re-scan. Pro is $9 once, there is no renewal, and the resume never leaves the browser tab.

What the free scan actually shows

The free report gives you a 0–100 score, four category scores, the top five missing keywords, parser warnings, and the first four fixes. Pro adds every missing keyword ranked by importance, the complete role checklist, every fix, a cover-letter outline, eight interview questions, and a downloadable text report. There is no subscription and no recurring charge.

Frequently asked questions

Is ResumeRadar a Resume Worded alternative?

For one task, yes: checking a resume against a specific job posting. ResumeRadar gives you a score, missing keywords, parser warnings, and fixes without an account or resume upload. Resume Worded is the better fit if you want AI rewriting, line-by-line feedback on the resume itself, LinkedIn review, and ATS templates inside an account.

How much does Resume Worded cost?

Resume Worded's Pro plan was listed at $49 per month, $99 every 3 months ($33 per month), or $229 for one year ($19 per month) when we checked on November 14, 2026. Resume Worded sets its own prices and they can change, so verify the current offer on its Pro page before buying.

Is Resume Worded free?

Resume Worded offers a free Score My Resume review with a score and the top issues holding the resume down. The full line-by-line analysis, AI rewriting, full Targeted Resume report, LinkedIn review, and templates are part of the paid Pro plan. The free review still requires an account and a PDF or DOCX upload.

Does ResumeRadar require an account?

No. You paste resume text and a job description, and the scan runs in your browser. There is no signup, no resume upload endpoint, and no server-side resume storage. Pro is a one-time $9 unlock, not a subscription.

Which one should I use?

Use Resume Worded if you want AI help rewriting the resume and a line-by-line review, and you are comfortable with a subscription plus a document upload. Use ResumeRadar if you already have resume text you trust and want one private, low-cost check of the posting in front of you.
