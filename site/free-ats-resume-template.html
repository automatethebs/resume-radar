How to use it

  1. Paste it into your editor. Google Docs, Word, or a plain-text editor all work. Keep one column and standard headings.
  2. Fill the summary last. Write the experience bullets first, then pull the strongest true theme into the summary.
  3. Put a number in every bullet you can. Revenue, time saved, volume, percentage, users, budget, error rate. If you do not have a number, describe the scope.
  4. Export a text-based PDF. Open the file and select the text. If it does not select, the parser cannot read it either.
  5. Tailor the top five terms. Use the free keyword extractor or a role guide, then run the scan against the actual posting.

What makes it ATS-safe

  • One column. Reading order stays predictable.
  • Standard headings. Parsers recognize Summary, Experience, Education, and Skills.
  • Contact details in the body. Not in a header or footer that may be skipped.
  • Month and year dates. The timeline is reconstructable.
  • Plain-text skills. No icons, bars, or ratings to misread.
  • Selectable text. The content survives the export.

Frequently asked questions

Is this resume template really free?

Yes. Copy it, download it, and use it without an account or email. The optional Pro report is a separate paid upgrade; the template itself is free.

How do I turn this template into a PDF?

Paste the filled template into Google Docs, Word, or another editor, keep the single-column layout, and export a text-based PDF. Use the print button on this page for a quick plain-text PDF.

Can I change the headings?

Keep the standard headings — Summary, Experience, Education, Skills — unless you have a strong reason to change them. They are anchors that parsers and recruiters both recognize.
