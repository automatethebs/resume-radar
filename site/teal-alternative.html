A lighter Teal alternative for one ATS check.

Teal is a resume builder and job-search platform with a genuinely useful free plan. ResumeRadar does one narrower job: it scores your resume against a specific posting, shows the keyword gaps, and explains the fixes — without an account, an upload, or a subscription.

The short version

If you want to build, store, and reuse resumes, Teal is the more complete product. It has designed templates, a free tier that never expires, and paid plans for unlimited analysis. If you want a private, inexpensive check of the posting in front of you, ResumeRadar is deliberately smaller: no account, no document storage, and a one-time price instead of a subscription.

When Teal is the better choice

Choose Teal if you are actively building resumes and want designed templates, a place to store multiple versions, and a free plan you can keep using indefinitely. Teal's free tier is unusually generous for a resume builder: 10 templates, top-5 keyword matching, and basic analysis at no cost. If those limits cover your search, you may not need to pay anyone. Teal+ makes sense if you want unlimited templates and analysis and you are applying often enough to justify a subscription.

When ResumeRadar is the better choice

Choose ResumeRadar if you already have a resume you like and need to know whether it matches one specific posting. There is no account to create, no document library to maintain, and no resume upload. The scan runs in your browser, and the $9 Pro unlock is a single payment rather than a recurring charge. It is also a practical fit for technical and Web3 roles, where the keyword lists are specific.

What the free scan actually shows

The free report gives you a 0–100 score, four category scores, the top five missing keywords, parser warnings, and the first four fixes. Pro adds every missing keyword ranked by importance, the complete role checklist, every fix, a cover-letter outline, eight interview questions, and a downloadable text report. There is no subscription and no recurring charge.

Frequently asked questions

Is ResumeRadar a Teal alternative?

For one task, yes: checking a resume against a specific job posting. ResumeRadar gives you a score, missing keywords, parser warnings, and fixes without an account or resume upload. Teal is the better fit if you want to build and store multiple resumes, use designed templates, and manage a broader job search inside an account.

How much does Teal cost?

Teal advertises a Free Forever plan. Teal+ was listed at $13 per week, $29 per 30 days, or $79 per 90 days when we checked on November 14, 2026. Teal sets its own prices and they can change, so verify the current offer on Teal's pricing page before buying.

What does Teal include for free?

At the time of checking, Teal's free plan included 10 resume templates, top-5 keyword matching, and basic resume analysis. Teal+ unlocked unlimited templates, keyword matching, and analysis. Check Teal's site for the current limits, because plan details change.

Does ResumeRadar require an account?

No. You paste resume text and a job description, and the scan runs in your browser. There is no signup, no resume upload endpoint, and no server-side resume storage. Pro is a one-time $9 unlock, not a subscription.

Which one should I use?

Use Teal if you want a resume builder with templates and an account that stores your documents, and you are comfortable with a subscription for unlimited analysis. Use ResumeRadar if you want one private, low-cost check of the posting in front of you and would rather not create another account.
