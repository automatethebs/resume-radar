How to use it

Run the checklist on the resume you are about to submit, not a master copy. Fix parsing and keyword problems first because they decide whether a human ever sees the rest. Then tighten the evidence and do the final pass. Re-scan after edits so you can see which change actually moved the score.

1. Parsing and format

An ATS has to read the file before it can score it. Fix these first: a resume that parses badly loses before keyword matching starts.

2. Keywords and targeting

Match the posting's language where it is true. The goal is coverage with evidence, not a wall of terms.

3. Evidence and impact

Recruiters scan for outcomes. Turn duties into evidence of scope, judgment, and results.

4. Readability and final pass

The last pass catches the errors that make a strong resume look careless.

What to fix first

  1. Parsing failures. If the contact block, headings, or dates cannot be read, nothing else matters.
  2. Missing top requirements. Cover the posting's most important terms where they are true, then show them inside experience bullets.
  3. Generic bullets. Replace duties with outcomes, scope, and numbers. This is usually the largest score gain after keywords.
  4. The final read. Proofread names, dates, and metrics, then scan the exact file you plan to send.

Frequently asked questions

How long does this ATS checklist take?

A first pass takes about 10–15 minutes. Once the resume is in shape, the final pre-submit pass usually takes five minutes per application.

Do I need to check every box?

No. A few items do not apply to every career or posting. Fix parsing and keyword coverage first: those checks decide whether the rest of the resume is read at all.

Does the checklist save my progress?

Yes, but only in this browser. The checked boxes are stored in localStorage on your device; nothing is uploaded or sent to ResumeRadar. Clearing site data resets the list.

Is this checklist affiliated with an ATS vendor?

No. ResumeRadar is an independent, client-side checker. This checklist is general resume advice, not a guarantee of passing any particular applicant tracking system.

What should I do after the checklist?

Scan the resume against the actual posting, fix the top three gaps it finds, and re-scan. Use the checklist again before you submit the next application.
