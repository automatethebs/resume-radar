Payment questions

Before you buy.

Is this a subscription?

No. It is a single payment. You get the Pro report on the device where you enter the code, and you can reuse it as often as you like. There is nothing to cancel.

What happens after I pay?

With a card, you are redirected back with an unlock code and Pro turns on automatically. With USDC on Base, paste the transaction hash into the unlock dialog; it is verified against the Base network in your browser and saved on that device.

Can I use the code on another device?

Yes — enter the same code on any device. Treat it like a license key: do not post it publicly.

What is your refund policy?

If the report does not work for you, open a support issue within 14 days and you will get a refund. USDC refunds are returned to the sending address. The free scan is there precisely so you can judge the quality before paying.

Do you store my resume or payment details?

No resume text is uploaded or stored. Payments are handled by the payment provider; this site never sees or stores card details. See the privacy page for the full list.
