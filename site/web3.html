Questions

The honest answers.

Do I need crypto to use the free checker?

No. The scan is free and does not require a wallet, account, or payment. USDC is only used if you choose to unlock the optional Pro report.

Why pay with USDC instead of a card?

USDC on Base settles without a card network, a billing account, or a KYC handoff to this site. It keeps the product usable for people who prefer not to attach a card to another job-search tool. Card checkout can be enabled by the operator, but it is not required.

Does ResumeRadar upload my resume?

No. The scoring engine runs in your browser. Resume and job-description text never leave the tab, and there is no server-side resume storage.

Is this only for crypto jobs?

No. There are 29 role guides covering healthcare, education, sales, finance, engineering, design, operations, and more. The Web3 guides are an addition, not a replacement.
