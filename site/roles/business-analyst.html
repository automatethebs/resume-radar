What an ATS looks for in a Business Analyst resume

Most applicant tracking systems reduce your resume to structured fields: contact details, job titles, employers, dates, skills, and education. For Business Analysts, the searchable layer is dominated by requirements gathering, business requirements, stakeholder management, process improvement. If those ideas appear only in a graphic, a table, or an image-based PDF, the parser may never see them.

  • Exact vocabulary. If the posting says “requirements gathering”, use that phrase rather than a clever variation. The checker treats common synonyms as partial matches, but recruiters search literal terms.
  • Evidence, not adjectives. “Strong business requirements skills” is claimed by everyone. A bullet with a number, a scope, and an outcome is believed.
  • Tools in context. List jira, confluence, visio in your skills block, then show where you used them inside a role. The combination is what survives both the parser and the skim.

How to use these keywords honestly

Pick the 8–12 terms that genuinely describe your last few years. Put the most important ones in your summary and your most recent role, because that is where both parsers and humans spend the most attention. If a required tool is missing, do not invent it — either close the gap with a small project or apply to roles where it is a nice-to-have rather than a hard filter.

Common Business Analyst resume mistakes

  • A skills list with no proof. Naming jira and confluence is not the same as showing what you built, analysed, or improved with them.
  • Duties instead of outcomes. “Responsible for stakeholder management” describes a job description, not a person. Start with a verb and end with a result.
  • One resume for every posting. The highest-leverage edit is matching the top five terms of each posting. It takes four minutes and changes the filter result.

Frequently asked questions

What keywords should a Business Analyst resume include?

Start with the core skills hiring managers scan for: requirements gathering, business requirements, stakeholder management, process improvement, gap analysis, sql. Then add the tools you actually use, such as jira, confluence, visio, excel, tableau. Mirror the exact wording of each job posting where it is truthful — a synonym can miss a keyword filter.

How do I get a Business Analyst resume past an ATS?

Use a single-column layout, plain text headings, month-and-year date ranges, and a dedicated skills section. Put the posting's most important terms inside experience bullets, not only in a list. Quantify outcomes, keep the file to one or two pages, and submit a PDF or DOCX that preserves selectable text.
